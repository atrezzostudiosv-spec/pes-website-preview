PES / CAREERS / 02

Journeyman Electrician

Carry out electrical installations, system upgrades and troubleshooting on residential and commercial projects.

Historical role profile · Los Angeles, CA · Previously listed as full-time. Current vacancy and terms are not confirmed.

01 / PES

About PES

We specialize in luxury and custom home builds, collaborate with industry teams and invest in employee training and development.

02 / PES

Responsibilities

Install new electrical systems, replace or upgrade panels and perform partial rewiring. Locate shorts and other faults using test equipment; replace failed elements, fuses and wiring. Install fixtures, conduits, motors and equipment; inspect circuit shielding and grounding. Use personal and company-provided equipment responsibly and follow safety standards.

03 / PES

Requirements

Demonstrate the practical installation and troubleshooting capabilities described above, together with knowledge of the NEC, local codes and construction safety. Bring personal hand tools; larger equipment is provided. Dependability, a strong work ethic and teamwork are expected.

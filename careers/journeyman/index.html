Company Overview

Premium Energy Solutions, Inc. is a well-established electrical company that has grown from humble beginnings to become a leader in the Los Angeles area. Specializing in luxury and custom home builds, we collaborate with top teams and builders in the industry. By utilizing the most advanced lighting technology and electrical design available today, we ensure exceptional service for our valued customers. We also prioritize the training and growth of our employees, fostering a supportive and dynamic work environment.

Job Summary

Premium Energy Solutions, Inc. is seeking experienced Journeyman Electricians for immediate openings. We are looking for dependable and hardworking team players who have their own hand tools; larger equipment will be provided, hardworking team players who are ready to contribute to our high standards of excellence.

Responsibilities

  • Perform construction and installation of electrical systems for residential/commercial projects.
  • Handle panel upgrades/replacements, partial rewires, and new installations.
  • Diagnose and repair shorts and failures (troubleshooting).
  • Repair malfunctions by replacing burned-out elements, fuses, and defective wiring.
  • Install fixtures, wiring conduits, motors, and other electrical equipment.
  • Inspect circuits for specified shielding and grounding.
  • Use personal and company-supplied tools and equipment responsibly.
  • Perform all job duties following established safety standards.
  • Locate and determine electrical malfunctions using test equipment.
  • Demonstrate knowledge of the National Electrical Code (NEC), local codes, and construction safety requirements.

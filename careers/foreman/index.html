PES / CAREERS / 01

Electrical Foreman — Residential

Lead electrical work on custom residential projects and support the crew in delivering work to PES standards.

Historical role profile · Los Angeles, CA · Previously listed as full-time. Current vacancy and terms are not confirmed.

01 / PES

About PES

We specialize in luxury and custom home builds, collaborate with industry teams and invest in employee training and development.

02 / PES

Responsibilities

Manage electrical projects against schedules and budgets; coordinate with builders, architects and stakeholders. Lead, train and support electricians; maintain a safe, organized site and follow applicable regulations. Use company-provided digital tools, including iPads and smartphones, to support project execution. Apply NEC, local code and construction safety knowledge.

03 / PES

Requirements

Proven foreman experience on luxury and custom homes; knowledge of lighting technology and electrical design; leadership, communication and teamwork. Bring personal hand tools; larger equipment is provided. Reliability, work ethic, basic computer and writing skills, valid driver’s license and clean driving record. Uphold integrity, accountability, teamwork, innovation and growth. HomeWorks knowledge preferred.

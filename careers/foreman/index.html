Location: Los Angeles, CA (Multiple locations)

Position: Full Time

Company Overview

Premium Energy Solutions, Inc. is a well-established electrical company that has grown from humble beginnings to become a leader in the Los Angeles area. Specializing in luxury and custom home builds, we collaborate with top teams and builders in the industry. By utilizing the most advanced lighting technology and electrical design available today, we ensure exceptional service for our valued customers. We also prioritize the training and growth of our employees, fostering a supportive and dynamic work environment.

Job Summary

Premium Energy Solutions, Inc. is seeking experienced Electrical Foremen to join our team immediately. The ideal candidates will be dependable, hardworking team players who are ready to contribute to our high standards of excellence.

Responsibilities

  • Oversee and manage electrical projects, ensuring they are completed on time and within budget.
  • Coordinate with builders, architects, and other stakeholders to ensure seamless project execution.
  • Lead a team of electricians, providing guidance, training, and support as needed.
  • Ensure all work is performed in compliance with local, state, and federal regulations.
  • Maintain a safe and organized work environment.
  • Utilize company-provided technology, such as iPads and smartphones, to ensure projects are completed on time and exceed customer expectations.
  • Demonstrate knowledge of the National Electrical Code (NEC), local codes, and construction safety requirements.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as an Electrical Foreman in luxury and custom home builds.
  • Strong knowledge of advanced lighting technology and electrical design.
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented environment.
  • Must have own hand tools; larger equipment will be provided.
  • Reliable and dependable with a strong work ethic.
  • Basic computer and writing skills.
  • Uphold all Premium Energy Solutions’ core values, including integrity, accountability, teamwork, innovation, and growth.
  • Valid driver's license and clean driving record.
  • Knowledge of lighting systems (Homeworks) preferred.

OUR STORY / LOS ANGELES

2007

Established in 2007, Premium Energy Solutions has grown from a small electrical company into a contractor serving Los Angeles and surrounding communities. We specialize in luxury and custom home builds, working with builders and professional teams in the industry.

OUR MISSION

Our mission.

We aim to deliver exceptional electrical service for residential and commercial properties at competitive rates. We focus on the needs of each client, from the design and installation of electrical systems to the lighting technology that brings a space to life.

01 / PES

Understanding Power Quality

Power quality describes how closely the voltage, current and frequency supplied to equipment meet the conditions that equipment needs. Changes in those conditions can influence the way connected systems operate.

02 / PES

Electrical Stability and Reliability

Voltage dips, interruptions and other disturbances can affect lighting, electronics and electrical equipment. Their causes and effects vary by property and connected load. A sound discussion starts with the existing installation and the demands placed on it.

03 / PES

Understanding Energy Management

Energy management means understanding where and when electricity is used, and planning for demand as a property evolves. That perspective can inform decisions about capacity, controls and future electrical requirements.

04 / PES

Modern Electrical Infrastructure

A property’s electrical infrastructure connects distribution, wiring, lighting and equipment. Remodels and new construction create opportunities to consider how those elements work together and what the property will need over time.

Technical reference: IEEE Technology Navigator, Power Quality.

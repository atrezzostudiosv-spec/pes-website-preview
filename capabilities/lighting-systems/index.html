PES / ELECTRICAL SERVICES

System lighting
upgrade.

As electrical and lighting technology evolves, older systems may no longer meet the needs of a home or business. PES helps you consider upgrades to electrical capacity, wiring and lighting controls when planning changes to your property.

Explore the service ↓

01 / PES

More power, more possibilities

Additional appliances, equipment and lighting can place new demands on an existing electrical system. A panel upgrade may be part of the solution when the existing capacity is not sufficient for the property’s requirements.

02 / PES

Safety and stability

A reliable electrical supply is important for the safe operation and longevity of appliances and equipment. Discuss your existing installation and planned changes so the electrical requirements can be addressed as part of the project.

03 / PES

Plan for future needs

A remodel offers an opportunity to consider quality wiring and structured wiring for the devices and features you expect to use. Modernizing infrastructure can help prepare the property for changing technology.

04 / PES

Smarter lighting controls

An updated lighting control system can offer new ways to manage the light in your home or business. Explore lighting design and installation if your upgrade also includes fixtures or controls.

05 / PES

Discuss your upgrade

Tell us what you want to improve, whether it is an older electrical panel, wiring during a remodel or the lighting control system.

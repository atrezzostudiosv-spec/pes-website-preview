Automated Window Coverings

Natural light shapes the way a room looks and feels. Premium Energy Solutions helps you plan automated window treatments based on your goals, from simple installations to more sophisticated requests.

Explore the service ↓

01 / PES

Manage natural light

Adjust the amount of daylight entering your home according to the space and your preferences. Automated coverings can make it easier to manage changing light conditions throughout the day.

02 / PES

Privacy and energy awareness

Window treatments help manage privacy while controlling incoming light. By managing daylight, they can also support energy efficiency; the results depend on the property and how the system is used.

03 / PES

Lutron shading systems

Through our partnership with Lutron shading systems, PES offers window treatment solutions suited to different installation requirements, including more complex and refined spaces.

04 / PES

Designed around your goals
